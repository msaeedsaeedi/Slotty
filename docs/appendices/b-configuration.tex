\chapter{Configuration reference}\label{app:config}

Every setting is an environment variable read at run time. Locally they come from \code{.env}
(\src{.env.example}); on AWS from the task definitions in \src{infra/aws/app/ecs.tf}, with secrets
from SSM (\cref{ch:aws}).

{\tablefont
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}P{40mm} P{22mm} L@{}}
  \toprule
  \thead{Variable} & \thead{On AWS} & \thead{Purpose}\\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  \envvar{DATABASE_URL} & SSM & PostgreSQL connection. On AWS: \code{sslmode=verify-full}; for the migrate task, the Prisma form.\\
  \envvar{DATABASE_POOL_MAX} & env (10) & Maximum connections per process.\\
  \envvar{APP_URL} & env & Public base URL for links in emails and calendar files; also the host the origin lock restores. Required in production.\\
  \envvar{ORIGIN_VERIFY_SECRET} & SSM & Secret header CloudFront sends; unset disables the origin lock.\\
  \envvar{SMTP_HOST}, \envvar{SMTP_PORT} & env & SES SMTP endpoint, port 587. Required (with \envvar{MAIL_FROM}) in a deployment: a production build with a public \envvar{APP_URL} refuses to start without it. Empty host is for local development only: emails are marked sent and noted in the worker log by recipient and subject, never their body.\\
  \envvar{SMTP_USER}, \envvar{SMTP_PASS} & SSM & SES SMTP credentials (worker only).\\
  \envvar{MAIL_FROM} & env & Sender, \code{Uniflow <no-reply@<domain>>}. Required in a deployment.\\
  \envvar{ENABLE_DEV_MAIL} & env & \code{1} shows \route{/dev/mail} in a production build, for the local e2e server only. A deployment refuses to start with it set, and never shows the page.\\
  \envvar{VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY}, \envvar{VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY} & SSM & Web Push keys; both empty turns push off.\\
  \envvar{VAPID_SUBJECT} & env & Contact for push services (\code{mailto:}).\\
  \envvar{ANALYTICS_SECRET} & SSM & Key for the pseudonymous visitor code. Unset turns usage analytics off (development and tests); feedback and surveys still work.\\
  \envvar{APP_VERSION} & deploy & The release recorded with events, feedback and survey answers; \src{infra/aws/deploy.sh} sets it to the image tag. Falls back to the package version.\\
  \envvar{NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS} & env & The RDS CA bundle in the image, for verified database TLS.\\
  \envvar{SSL_CERT_FILE} & image & Migrate image only: OpenSSL's trust store, set to the RDS CA bundle.\\
  \envvar{HOSTNAME}, \envvar{PORT} & env / image & Web server bind address (\code{0.0.0.0}) and port (3000).\\
  \envvar{WORKER_INTERVAL_MS} & default & Poll interval when \code{LISTEN} is unavailable (15\,s).\\
  \envvar{WORKER_SCHEDULE_MS} & default & How often scheduled jobs run (60\,s).\\
  \envvar{ADMIN_EMAIL}, \envvar{ADMIN_NAME}, \envvar{ADMIN_PASSWORD} & one-off & Inputs to \code{create-admin.js}.\\
  \envvar{NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_SW} & --- & Development only: register the service worker under \code{next dev}.\\
  \envvar{ENABLE_DEV_MAIL} & --- & Development and end-to-end tests: enable \route{/dev/mail}.\\
  \envvar{NEXT_DIST_DIR} & --- & End-to-end tests: separate build directory.\\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}}
